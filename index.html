Main Features

1.

Light­weight & Opti­mized.

Our average medium asset is just 50 KB in size. The whole library is currently only around 11 MB, which makes it ideal for kitbashing or for direct use in games.

2.

One. Simple. Fee.

We hate subscriptions. We adore software that you can buy once and actually own. The license covers unlimited commercial use for concepts and bespoke 3D scenes, asset assembly, and environments. More information can be found in the FAQ.

3.

Built for Blender.

We love Blender as much as you do. Proper naming and group structure, an integrated asset library, and efficient procedural materials are all ready to drag and drop.

4.

No AI.

You want to build concepts, visualizations, animations, and games without AI, and we have you covered. All assets here are built by real humans.
